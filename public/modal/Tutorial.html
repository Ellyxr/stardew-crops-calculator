Page Overview
The site is made up of four main sections:
Crop Calculator
Here you can add crops using two methods, Single Field or Multiple Fields. You’ll enter basic info like:
Crop Name
Seed Price and Crop Price
Growth Days and Regrowth time
Yield and Crop Category type
Only the Crop name, Prices, and Growth days are required but adding Yield and Category can help to make your results more accurate.
Single Field Crop Format Guide
Name SeedPrice CropPrice GrowthDays YieldType(a|b|c) CropCategory(f|v|m) Regrows?(yes/no) EveryWhatDays

YieldType: a = Single Yield, b = Multiple (random amount), c = Multiple (fixed per harvest)
Crop Category: f = fruit, v = vegetable, m = mushroom
Both input types work the same! The difference is just how you enter the data.
Graph Container
Once you submit your crops, they’ll appear on both the and List and the Graph.
- Hover over any bar to see its details in the Crop Details panel.
- Toggle artisan goods to see the processed value of each crop. If the graph doesn’t refresh automatically, click “Refresh Graph.”
- Use Advanced Settings to adjust farm settings and click Submit to update the graph.
List Container
This is where all your added crops are displayed. You can:
- Export your current crop list and Import it later (no need to re-enter everything!)
- Search crops in real time.
Edit or delete crops directly:
To delete: select one or more crops and press Delete.
To edit yield type or category: just click and update — no need to enter edit mode.
To edit other details: click Edit, make your changes, then Save Edit and close the modal.
List Container
Each bar on the graph represents one crop. Hovering over a bar shows its detailed stats below. Each part means:
Harvest Summary
Name Meaning
Total Profit How much gold you’ll earn in total after subtracting seed costs.
ROI Percent “Return on Investment.” The higher the number, the more gold you’re getting back for what you spent.
Total Profit How much gold you’ll earn in total after subtracting seed costs.
Profit per Day Average daily profit over the season or duration.
Crops Sold Total number of crops harvested and sold during the season.
Crop Quality & Value
Name Meaning
Normal / Silver / Gold / Iridium Show the base selling price per quality tier.
Expected AVG The weighted average price per crop, factoring in your fertilizer and farming level chances.
Adjusted Value The expected average after penalties (if any) or small rounding adjustments.
Seed & Sell Prices
Name Meaning
Seed Cost The price you pay per seed (or single plant).
Base Crop Price The base selling price before any quality or skill bonuses.
Total Revenue All gold earned from every harvest combined.
Total Cost The total gold spent on seeds for that season.
Growth & Harvest Info
Name Meaning
Growth Days How many days it takes for the first harvest to grow.
Harvests per Season How many full harvests you can get within one 28-day season.
Break-Even Point How many harvests it takes to make back what you spent on seeds.
Regrows Every For crops that regrow. It shows how many days between harvests.
About This Website
This tool helps Stardew Valley players, especially modded farm enjoyers, track and maximize crop profits. It supports both vanilla and modded crops, with tax support in the making.

I built this with players who use multiple modded crops in mind as it can be tricky to decide which crops to plant when using several expansion mods. This tool aims to help you plan your farm with the same precision passion you put into your layouts!
